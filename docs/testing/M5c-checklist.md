# OpenDrape M5c: what to try

This build replaces the human avatar with a **dress form** on its stand, and adds an **Assets**
section where you pick the form (women's or men's torso), its size chart and size, or type
your own measurements. Your choice is saved with the project.

## Check these

- [ ] **The dress form.** The 3D view shows a beige torso on a charcoal stand, with thin dark
      tape lines. There is no human body any more.
- [ ] **Assets.** Click **Assets** in the menu row (after View). The list of dress forms takes
      the place of the pattern on the right. Close it three ways: click Assets again, click the
      ✕ at the top right, or pick a tab (Modeling, Finishing…).
- [ ] **3D | 2D.** While Assets is open in Modeling, a **3D | 2D** switch sits at the top of the
      left area. Click **2D**: the pattern table moves there, and you can draw with the Pen (H).
      Click **3D** to bring the 3D view back. Closing Assets puts everything back in its place.
- [ ] **Pictures.** Each form's card shows a small picture of it in the studio light.
- [ ] **Men's torso.** Click its card: the form changes. **Cmd+Z** brings the women's form
      back, and **Cmd+Shift+Z** the men's again. The title shows the project is unsaved.
- [ ] **Chart and size.** Click **Everyday body**: the size nearest yours is picked. Open the
      **Size** list and pick **US 14 / UK 18**: the form grows.
- [ ] **Custom measurements.** Open **Custom measurements**, click **Waist**, type 72 and press
      Enter: the waist changes, and the size list says Custom. Type 200 and press Enter: a
      message says what the waist can be, and nothing changes. Under the fields you see what
      the form measures (front waist length, apex to apex, high hip).
- [ ] **Show tape lines.** Untick it: the tape lines go. Quit and reopen: still off.
- [ ] **Going up sizes.** Draw a skirt's front and back, use Place at → Front and → Back, then
      pick **US 18**: no piece ends up inside the bigger form. Press Play: it drapes.
- [ ] **T-shirt sleeves.** Place a sleeve with Place at → **Left armhole**. It starts beside the
      armhole and, when draped, hangs down from it as on a real dress form (there are no arms).
- [ ] **Saving.** Save, close and reopen the file: the same form and size come back. An older
      project opens on the women's torso, Classic chart, US 8.

If something looks wrong, use **Help → About OpenDrape → Copy diagnostics** and paste it
into your message with a screenshot.
