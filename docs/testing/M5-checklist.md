# OpenDrape M5: what to try

This build organises OpenDrape into five **workspaces**, shown as tabs along the top:
**Modeling · Finishing · Texturing · Rendering · Animation**. Everything you could do before now
lives in **Modeling**. The other four tabs say what they will hold; their tools come in later
updates. Nothing you draft, sew or drape behaves differently.

## Check these

- [ ] **The tabs.** The top bar shows File, Edit, View and Help on the left, and the five tabs
      in the middle. **Modeling** is highlighted.
- [ ] **Click each tab.** The 3D view stays on the left the whole time. Finishing, Texturing,
      Rendering and Animation each show their name, one line about what they will hold, and
      "Coming in a later update." Modeling shows the pattern table again.
- [ ] **Keys.** Press **Cmd+1** to **Cmd+5** (Ctrl on Windows): each opens its tab in order.
- [ ] **View menu.** Open **View**: it lists the five tabs with their keys. Pick one; it opens.
- [ ] **The tool strip.** In Modeling, the pattern tools are icons in a column down the left
      of the pattern table. Hover each one: its name and key show (for example "Pen (H)"),
      with a line about what it does. Click one, or press its letter: it lights up.
- [ ] **Above the table:** cm / inch, Show lengths, Show seam allowance and Fit (Cmd+0) are in
      a slim bar.
- [ ] **3D buttons.** Play and Reset are icons now. Hover them: each says what it does.
- [ ] **Undo everywhere.** Draw a piece in Modeling. Open Texturing (Cmd+3) and press **Cmd+Z**:
      the piece goes from the 3D view. **Shift+Cmd+Z** brings it back.
- [ ] **Letters only in Modeling.** In Texturing, press **H**, then go back to Modeling: the
      tool hasn't changed.
- [ ] **No false "unsaved".** Open a saved project and click through all five tabs: the window
      title doesn't get a "•".
- [ ] **Short screen.** Make the window small and select a piece: the Properties panel on the
      right scrolls instead of cutting off.
- [ ] **Dark mode.** Switch your Mac to dark mode: everything is readable, and the selected
      tool and tab are highlighted in blue.
- [ ] **Same as before.** Draft, sew and drape the T-shirt from the M4b checklist: everything
      works as it did.

If something looks wrong, use **Help → About OpenDrape → Copy diagnostics** and paste it
into your message with a screenshot.
